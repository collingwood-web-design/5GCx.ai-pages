Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

5GCx (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through 5gcx.ai, how we use it, and the choices you have. 5GCx is a Canadian company and handles personal information in line with applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Who we are

5GCx
Canada

Email: amondou@5GCx.ai

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you choose to give us, and limited technical information that is created when you use the site.

Email. This website does not have a contact form. If you email a member of our team directly, we keep the information you share, such as your name, email address and message, so we can respond.

Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery providers may automatically receive standard request data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and timestamps. We do not use this to build marketing profiles about you.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries you send us
  • Operate, secure and improve this website
  • Meet legal or professional obligations when required

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share personal information only when needed to run this website or when the law requires it:

  • Hosting and media. Site files and images are served through hosting and content delivery network (CDN) providers.
  • Fonts. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts are requested.
  • Legal requests. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set its own advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s storage for two small settings that contain no personal information:

  • One remembers that you have already seen the opening animation during your visit. It is cleared when you close the browser tab.
  • One remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice, so it is not shown again.

Third-party services we use, such as Google Fonts, may set or read cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. You can clear or block cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

Retention

We keep email inquiries for as long as needed to respond, keep business records and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it when practical.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please avoid sending highly sensitive information by email.

Your rights

Subject to Canadian privacy law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent
  • Delete information we no longer need to retain

To make a request, email amondou@5GCx.ai. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party websites, articles and social profiles, such as LinkedIn and Billie Flynn – My Blog. Their privacy practices are their own. We encourage you to read their policies before sharing personal information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
