5GCx is an AI/ML driven defence-tech & dual-use platform poised to redefine 5th Gen fighter lead-in training. The innovation – Sensor Fusion Capacity™. In an era of unprecedented global security challenges, the demand for advanced military training capabilities has never been more critical.

THE 5GCx MISSION

To accelerate the 5th Gen pilot’s operational readiness, successfully execute in a highly complex, data-driven battlespace, and ultimately train for lethality. 5GCx is a strategic fit with Canada’s F-35 procurement, NORAD and NATO interoperable technology that can be deployed across allied nations.

Aligned with Canada’s defence policy and commitment to tech & data sovereignty, 5GCx supports the modernization of military capabilities and the resilience of critical infrastructure across the RCAF/CAF and allied operations.
